Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Resident Questions

What is the pet policy at Nestora Living? +
We love pets! We allow up to 2 cats or dogs per residence with no breed restrictions. A one-time refundable pet deposit of $250 applies.
What are the lease duration options? +
We offer flexible lease terms ranging from 6 to 18 months, with standard 12-month leases offering the best monthly rate.
Is parking included with the residence? +
Reserved underground heated parking is available with EV charging stations for $120/month per vehicle.
