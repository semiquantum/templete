Welcome to Oakwood

Nurturing Confident Learners Since 1989

Oakwood Academy is a community dedicated to inspiring young minds to achieve their highest personal and academic potential. We nurture curiosity, instill integrity, and build a strong foundation for lifelong learning.

Our student-centric approach ensures individual attention, encouraging students to question, explore, and innovate in a safe and supportive learning environment.

"Education is not just the filling of a bucket, but the lighting of a fire. We ignite the passion for learning in every child."

Dr. Margaret Hayes

Principal & Educational Director

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common queries regarding admissions, transportation, and school policies.

Admissions require a completed application form, previous academic report cards, birth certificate, and a brief interactive assessment/interview with our academic coordinator.

Yes, we operate a fleet of GPS-tracked, air-conditioned school buses with trained safety attendants serving all major residential routes in the metropolitan area.

We maintain an optimal student-to-teacher ratio of 15:1 to ensure every child receives personalized attention and tailored academic guidance.

Oakwood provides merit-based academic scholarships and need-based financial bursaries. Applications can be submitted directly during the admission inquiry process.

Campus safety is our top priority. We feature 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric access gates, trained security staff, and a dedicated full-time medical nurse on duty.
