Patient Resources

Patient Information & Guide

Everything you need to know before visiting MedCare, from documentation checklists to insurance coverage and visiting policies.

Arrival Guidelines

  • Arrive 15 minutes prior to scheduled appointment.
  • Check-in at the outpatient reception with your booking ID.
  • Wear comfortable loose clothing if physical exam is required.

Fasting & Lab Tests

  • Overnight 8-10 hour fasting for lipid profile and glucose tests.
  • Continue taking regular blood pressure medications with water.
  • Inform the nurse of any allergies or current medications.

Hygiene & Safety

  • Sanitization stations are available at all corridor entries.
  • Masks are recommended if you have flu-like symptoms.
  • Wheelchair assistance available at main portico entrance.

Government ID & Registration

  • Government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar, Passport, Driver's License).
  • MedCare Patient Registration Card (for returning patients).
  • Digital confirmation SMS or email copy.

Medical History Records

  • Prior X-rays, MRI scans, CT reports, and lab findings.
  • Current prescription list with exact dosages.
  • Discharge summaries from previous hospitalizations.

Referral Documents

  • Physician referral letters or specialist consultation notes.
  • Pre-authorization approval letters for corporate health plans.
  • Immunization booklet (for pediatric consultations).

General Ward Visiting

  • Morning: 10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
  • Evening: 04:30 PM – 07:30 PM
  • Maximum 2 visitors permitted at the bedside at a time.

ICU & Critical Care Units

  • Morning: 11:00 AM – 11:30 AM
  • Evening: 05:00 PM – 05:30 PM
  • 1 immediate family member only to maintain sterile protocol.

Emergency Department

  • Open 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
  • 1 companion allowed in the emergency observation cubicle.
  • Immediate triage without registration delay.

Cashless Health Insurance

  • Empanelled with 30+ leading insurance providers and TPAs.
  • Dedicated on-site TPA desk for instant pre-authorization.
  • Bring original insurance health card and policy document.

Payment Methods

  • Accepts UPI, Debit/Credit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash.
  • 0% EMI payment options available for major procedures.
  • Itemized, transparent invoices with no hidden fees.

Billing Assistance

  • Financial counselors available for procedure cost estimations.
  • Corporate desk for corporate tie-ups and employee healthcare.
  • Online bill view and receipt downloads available.
