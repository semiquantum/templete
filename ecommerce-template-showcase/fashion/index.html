The Cashmere Capsule
Editorial Story

Handcrafted in Biella & Florence

Each garment in the Autumn / Winter capsule is born from century-old family mills in Northern Italy. We blend rare 15.2-micron Grade-A Mongolian cashmere with pure French silk for an ethereal hand-feel that only improves with time.

Our tailoring philosophy honors architectural drape and effortless wearability—designed not for fleeting seasons, but for enduring heirloom wardrobes.
