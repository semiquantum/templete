Technical Capabilities

Shared Architecture & Features

All 5 stores share a clean, high-performance modular frontend foundation while presenting unique brand aesthetics.

Shopping Cart Engine

Slide-out cart drawer with quantity adjustment, item removal, promo code discounts (SAVE15, LUXE20), tax, and free shipping progress bar with localStorage persistence.

Wishlist Persistence

Interactive heart toggle on all product cards with animated pop feedback, live navbar counter sync, and state persistence via browser storage.

Live Search & Autocomplete

Instant client-side debounced search matching product titles, descriptions, and categories, paired with header autocomplete dropdowns.

Faceted Filters & Sorting

Category pills, real-time price range slider, and 5-mode sort algorithms (Price Low/High, Rating, Discount, Newest) with zero page reloads.

Quick-View Product Modal

Interactive gallery thumbnail switcher, variant & color selectors, quantity stepper, and tabbed specifications with keyboard accessibility.

Mobile & Tablet Fluidity

Full responsiveness tested across 320px, 375px, 425px, 768px, 1024px, and 1440px with touch-friendly navigation drawers and 0 horizontal overflow.
