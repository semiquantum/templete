# Frontend Website Templates

A collection of professional, modern and responsive frontend website templates designed for client presentation and easy customization.

The templates are built with HTML5, CSS3 and Vanilla JavaScript (ES6), with a focus on clean structure, professional UI/UX, responsive layouts and functional frontend interactions.

## Included Templates

### Business Consulting

A professional consulting website focused on strategy, business growth, advisory services and client solutions.

### Business Agency

A modern agency website focused on creative services, digital strategy, branding and client results.

### Startup Company

A modern startup website focused on innovation, products, growth and business opportunities.

### Professional Services

A premium service-business website focused on expertise, consultation, solutions and client relationships.

### Corporate Business

An enterprise-oriented corporate website focused on business operations, services, leadership, performance and credibility.

### IT Company

An enterprise technology website focused on IT infrastructure, cloud solutions, cybersecurity, managed services and digital transformation.

### SaaS Platform

A product-focused SaaS website presenting software workflows, automation, analytics, productivity and collaboration.

### AI Company

A professional AI company website focused on artificial intelligence, automation, data intelligence, prediction and technology solutions.

### Software Company

An engineering-focused software website covering software development, platforms, APIs, architecture and engineering services.

### Technology Startup

A modern technology startup website focused on technology products, innovation, growth, product development and business value.

## UI/UX

- Professional visual hierarchy
- Clear typography
- Consistent spacing
- Intentional whitespace

## Responsive Design

The templates are designed to adapt across:

- Mobile phones
- Tablets
- Laptops
- Desktop screens
- Large desktop displays

Responsive layouts use CSS Grid, Flexbox, media queries and fluid sizing.

The layouts are designed to avoid:

- Horizontal overflow
- Clipped content
- Overlapping elements
- Broken navigation
- Broken cards
- Form overflow
- Footer overflow

## Accessibility

The templates use accessibility-conscious frontend practices including:

- Semantic HTML
- Keyboard-friendly navigation
- Visible focus states
- Accessible mobile navigation
- Form labels
- Appropriate ARIA states
- Reduced-motion support
- Accessible interactive components

## Interactive Functionality

Vanilla JavaScript provides frontend interactions such as:

- Mobile menu open/close
- FAQ interactions
- Form validation
- Error and success feedback
- Interactive UI components
- Scroll-based animations
- Internal navigation

Forms are frontend-only and do not send real messages without a backend or external service.

## Running a Template

No build process is required.

Open the template's `index.html` in a modern web browser.

Example:

```text
it-company/index.html
```

or:

```text
saas-platform/index.html
```
