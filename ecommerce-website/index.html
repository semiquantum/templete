OUR PROVENANCE

Built for Generations, Not Seasons.

Every object in the Meridian & Holt catalog is conceived through our architectural discipline: structural honesty, natural patinating materials, and zero disposable components.

We partner with fourth-generation joiners in Oregon, heritage brass spin-casters in Birmingham, and master ceramicists in Kyoto. Each production run is intentionally capped to maintain uncompromising quality control.

"True luxury is not ornament, but the silent perfection of joinery and the tactile warmth of honest materials."
